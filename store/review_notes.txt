Abandoned Dunes - App Review notes (Guideline 2.1 information, provided up front)

(a) Screen recording (real iPhone, native app, TestFlight build)
The attached screen recording (abandoned-dunes-app-review.mp4, about 1 min 50 s) was captured on a physical iPhone and has two parts:
Part 1 (0:00-1:09): Terrific Dunes (the free course), hole 1 and into hole 2. Fly-over of the hole, aim with the wind dial and club selector, the slingshot swing (drag back and release), ball flight, approach shots, putting on the green with the break/slope readout, and the start of hole 2.
Part 2 (1:09-1:50): the end of the free 5-hole teaser round (scorecard and "Unlock all 7 courses - $2.99" prompt), the paywall, the Apple in-app purchase sheet for "All Courses" (sandbox, not charged), the "You're all set" confirmation, the scorecard, then the course list with all courses now unlocked, difficulty selection, and the start of a hole on Abandon Trails (a previously locked course). Multiplayer (Play Friends, Game Center turn-based matches) and the Game Center leaderboards are not shown in the recordings; see (c) for how to reach them. Multiplayer needs two Game Center accounts.

(b) Purpose and audience
Abandoned Dunes is a single-player first-person 3D slingshot golf game with its own rendering and physics engine (three.js inside a native Capacitor shell). Seven parody-named links courses (Bandit Dunes, Terrific Dunes, Abandon Trails, Old MacDonald Had a Links, Sheep Wreck, Preserve Us!, Short Circuit), three difficulty levels, wind, haptics, Game Center leaderboards and achievements. Fully offline, no ads, no analytics, no accounts, no servers.

(c) How to access everything (no login or account is needed)
1. Free teaser: tap Play a Round > Terrific Dunes (badged FREE - HOLES 1-5) > pick a difficulty > Tee it up. Drag back on the screen like a slingshot and release to hit. Holes 1-5 of Terrific Dunes are free. After the 5th free hole the round-end screen offers the unlock.
2. Unlock: the other six courses are locked; tapping one (or "Unlock all 7 courses" on the main menu) opens the paywall for the single non-consumable in-app purchase "All Courses" (com.gorillastocks.abandoneddunes.fullgame, $2.99 launch price). It unlocks all 7 courses and all holes. Restore Purchases is on the main menu and on the paywall. No subscription.
3. Leaderboards: main menu > Leaderboards (Game Center; 21 leaderboards, 4 achievements). Scores are submitted only for completed full courses. Game Center sign-in is optional; the game works the same without it.
4. Multiplayer (iOS only): main menu > Play Friends. It uses Apple Game Center turn-based matches only (friends picker, auto-match, or an invite sent through Messages) for 2-4 players; one player at a time plays a round on the same course and wind seed, only stroke counts are exchanged. There is no chat, no user-generated content and no custom server. Testing a full match needs two Game Center accounts (sandbox accounts work). With a single account you can still open the match list and create a match or auto-match.

(d) External services
Only Apple services: StoreKit (the in-app purchase) and Game Center. No other network access.

(e) Regions
The app works the same in every region.

(f) Third-party material and trademarks
Course layouts use OpenStreetMap data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL, credited on the main menu) and public-domain USGS elevation and USDA NAIP data, all bundled with the app. Course names are parodies and the app states it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any real golf course or resort. The app is not in a regulated industry and contains no gambling.
