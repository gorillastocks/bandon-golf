Abandoned Dunes — Support

First-person slingshot golf across seven parody links courses.

Get help or report a bug

Open an issue on GitHub: github.com/gorillastocks/bandon-golf/issues. Please include your device model, iOS version and what you were doing when it happened.

How to play

  • Drag back on the screen like a slingshot, then release to hit. Pull farther for more power.
  • Use the club arrows to change clubs, the arrow buttons to adjust aim, and the target button to aim at the pin.
  • Watch the wind dial: it shows wind direction relative to your shot, and strength in mph.
  • The Menu button pauses, saves & quits, or changes graphics and sound options.

Common questions

The game runs slowly. Open the menu and set Graphics to Low or Medium, and turn Tournament crowd off.

Where is my saved round? Choose Save & Quit in the pause menu; Continue appears on the main menu. Saves live on your device only, so deleting the app deletes them.

Do you collect my data? No. See the privacy policy.

Is this affiliated with a real golf resort? No. Course names are parodies and all courses are stylized; the game is not affiliated with or endorsed by any real golf course or resort. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Bandon Dunes Golf Resort.
