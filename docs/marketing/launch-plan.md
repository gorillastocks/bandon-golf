# Launch plan (draft)

## Positioning
Pull-back slingshot golf on wind-whipped coastal links. Real wind, real-elevation terrain, seven parody courses, fully offline, no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases, Game Center leaderboards.
Trademark rule: never use real course or resort names in any copy, tags or keywords. Courses are "parody-named"; keep the disclaimer.

## Audience and hooks
- Casual golf-game players who like short rounds (Short Circuit = 9 par 3s, Preserve Us! = 13 par 3s).
- Mobile players tired of ads and energy timers: "No ads. No tracking. No IAP."
- Links-golf fans who recognise the coastal style: "wind is the hazard".
- Competitive players: 21 Game Center leaderboards (7 courses x 3 difficulties), 4 achievements.

| Phase | When | Actions | Needs approval |

| 0. Pre-launch | now until TestFlight build processed | finish listing, screenshots, privacy, review notes; TestFlight smoke test on a real iPhone (haptics, Game Center) | no |

| 4. Post-launch | weekly | read reviews, respond, track keyword ranking and competitor changes, ship a patch update with What's New copy | replies to reviews are posts: yes |

## Cadence (proposal; no routine is running)
- Weekly: competitor/keyword check (golf and slingshot-golf apps), review roundup, leaderboard sanity check.
- Monthly: ASO copy refresh (keywords field, promotional text rotates with no review needed).

## Budget
- Planned: $0 organic.
- Optional, only with Sean's approval: Apple Search Ads Basic at $5-10/day after the app is live and the Search Results ad variants are set up (same proposal as Blackspire).

## KPIs
Impressions to product page views to downloads (App Analytics), keyword rank for "golf", "slingshot golf", "golf wind", rating and review count, Game Center active players.

## Risks
- Guideline 4.2 (web-wrapper) review risk: mitigations are in `docs/APP_STORE.md`. Do not market before approval.
- Parody names: keep disclaimers; three commentary clips with real names are disabled, three more may speak real course names (`pacific_01`, `sheep_01`, `oldmac_01`): decide before using audio in promo.
- Account-level: Apple DSA trader status is unresolved on this developer account (EU availability may be blocked); do not advertise to the EU until resolved.
