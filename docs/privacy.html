Privacy Policy

Abandoned Dunes · Last updated: October 31, 2026

The short version: Abandoned Dunes does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party SDKs.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings (graphics level, sound, commentary and haptics preferences), your in-progress round, and your personal-best scores using the browser/app local storage on your own device. This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you delete the app (or clear the website's data in your browser).

Network access

The iOS app is fully offline: all courses, textures, audio and code ship inside the app. The web version at this site loads its own files from this site only. Neither version sends gameplay, usage or device data to us or to anyone else.

Apple Game Center (iOS app only)

The iOS app can optionally sign you in to Apple Game Center to show leaderboards and achievements. If you are signed in to Game Center, your final round scores and unlocked achievements are submitted to Apple's Game Center service and are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive your name, Apple ID or any other personal information. If you decline or are not signed in, the game works exactly the same and nothing is submitted.

Children

The game contains no ads, purchases, chat or user-generated content, and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Third-party data credits

Course layouts use map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), USGS elevation data and USDA NAIP aerial imagery, all bundled with the game. These are static files; no requests are made to those services while you play.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
