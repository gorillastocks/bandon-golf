Attribution is not required; credited here anyway. Only models labelled CC0 are used.

`crowd.bin.gz` / `crowd.json` are derived from those models by `tools/crowd/bake.py` (decimated LODs, vertex-role tagging, baked skinning
matrices). The idle / walk / run / hit clips are the packs' own animations; clap, cheer, groan, alert, duck and the seated variants are
procedural (IK) clips generated by `tools/crowd/clips.py`. Caps, visors and tinting are generated in code. The grandstand and ropes are procedural.
