# Aerial imagery credits

Hole textures in this folder are crops of **NAIP** (National Agriculture Imagery Program) orthoimagery, USDA Farm Service Agency,
acquired 2022-06-23 (quad m_4312453), retrieved from the USGS The National Map `USGSNAIPImagery` ImageServer.
NAIP imagery is produced by the US federal government and is in the **public domain** (no copyright, no licence conditions);
credit requested: "Imagery: USDA NAIP via USGS The National Map". Re-projected/cropped/recompressed to WebP only.
Pipeline: `tools/naip/`.
