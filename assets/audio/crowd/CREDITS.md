# Crowd reaction clips

Real recordings from [Freesound](https://freesound.org), all released under **CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)** — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . No attribution is required; credit is given here anyway.

The files here are trimmed excerpts of the Freesound 128 kbps MP3 previews of each sound, loudness-normalised (≈ -12…-14 LUFS, peak ≤ -0.1 dBFS via limiter), faded in/out, and re-encoded as 96 kbps stereo 44.1 kHz MP3 (`/workspace/src/build.py` recipe: ffmpeg `volume` + `alimiter` + `afade`).
