# Ambient ocean waves

`waves.mp3` — 39.6 s seamless loop of small waves lapping onto a beach, mono 32 kHz 80 kbps, loudness-normalised to about -20 LUFS (played at gain 0.65 on coastal holes only; wind is ducked 25% there). Processing is light: mono mix, 50 Hz high-pass, normalise, and a 1.5 s equal-power crossfade at the loop seam, which sits at a trough between waves (-31 dB at the loop end vs -30 dB at the start, so there is no level jump). No expansion or other dynamics processing.

Source: ["Small waves and beach #2"](https://bigsoundbank.com/small-waves-and-beach-2-s1448.html) (BigSoundBank sound no. 1448, "noise of small waves arriving on the beach, sound taken at 10 meters"; ORTF stereo, 48 kHz / 24 bit field recording) by Joseph Sardin, **CC0 / public domain**, no attribution required. Excerpt: 277.0–318.1 s of the 6:14 recording.
