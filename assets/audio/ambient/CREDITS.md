# Ambient ocean waves

`waves.mp3` — 30 s seamless loop (2 s equal-power crossfade at the seam), mono 32 kHz 64 kbps, high-passed at 60 Hz, with a downward expander so the troughs between waves are near-silent, loudness-normalised to ≈ -23 LUFS (played at low gain in-game, coastal holes only).

Source: [Waves on a sand bar](https://freesound.org/people/plantmonkey/sounds/459813/) by plantmonkey (excerpt 4–36 s of the Freesound preview) — **CC0 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), no attribution required.
